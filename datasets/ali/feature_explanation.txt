=== Feature Definitions and Calculation Methods ===
(19 features in total)
===================================================
n_reviews:
    Number of reviews written by the user.

prop_not_na:
    Proportion of reviews by the user that are not 'n' (not missing or placeholder).

mean_review_len:
    Mean length of the user's reviews (treating 'n' as length 0).

std_review_len:
    Standard deviation of the user's review lengths.

min_review_len:
    Minimum review length for the user.

max_review_len:
    Maximum review length for the user.

mean_price:
    Mean price of products reviewed by the user.

std_price:
    Standard deviation of product prices reviewed by the user.

min_price:
    Minimum price of products reviewed by the user.

max_price:
    Maximum price of products reviewed by the user.

mean_seller_age:
    Mean seller age (in days) at the time of review for products reviewed by the user.

std_seller_age:
    Standard deviation of seller age (in days) for products reviewed by the user.

min_seller_age:
    Minimum seller age (in days) for products reviewed by the user.

max_seller_age:
    Maximum seller age (in days) for products reviewed by the user.

MNR:
    Maximum number of reviews written by the user in a single day.

ANR:
    Average number of reviews written by the user per day (on days with at least one review).

NDR:
    Number of days in which the user wrote at least one review.

ENR:
    Entropy of the user's review activity over days (measures how evenly reviews are distributed across days).

MeanVar:
    Mean squared deviation of the user's ratings from the average product rating.

TMF:
    Trustworthiness measure: for products where the user gave the highest rating, how unique is the user among all such reviewers.

PopRank:
    Average popularity (number of reviews) of products reviewed by the user.

CatEnt:
    Entropy of the distribution of product categories reviewed by the user (measures diversity of categories).

BST:
    Number of days between the user's first and last review.
